Website review
Ten checks for a therapy website
Score each area from one to five. Anything below four is work for this month. Write the detail on page two.
01
First screen
In five seconds a visitor knows who you help and where you are based.
02
One clear action
Every page leads to one step. Book a free consultation, or send an enquiry.
03
Accreditation on show
Registration body and membership number sit in the header or footer, not buried.
04
Fees and format
Session price, length, and format are stated on the page. No enquiry required.
05
Words for the client
Copy opens with the client's experience. Training and history follow on the about page.
06
The first session
A short section describes what happens in the first meeting. This lowers the barrier to booking.
07
Crisis signposting
A visible line names the national crisis lines for urgent risk.
08
Privacy and data
Privacy notice covers session notes and data protection registration. Cookie notice is present.
09
Mobile, speed, contrast
Open it on a real phone. Tap targets are 44 pixels or taller. Images sit under 200 kilobytes.
10
Search basics
Each page carries a unique title and description. One page for each issue she works with.
