# Hakim Group one-page management report

## Voice
- HG-friendly voice. Plain English, reading age 9 to 11 ("explain like I am 10").
- No payroll jargon. Translate terms:
  - National Living Wage / NMW -> "legal minimum hourly pay"
  - Employer NI -> "National Insurance the company pays"
  - Employer pension -> "Pension the company pays"
  - Zero hours -> "flexible hours with no set hours"
  - Pay differentials -> "pay gaps between managers and their teams"
  - Posts / occupancies -> "jobs"
  - FTE, pro-rated, qualifying earnings, secondary threshold -> do not use
- Short sentences. Active voice. No contractions. No em dashes. No hashtags.
- Avoid: can, may, just, really, literally, actually, basically, hence, however, utilise, moreover, powerful, groundbreaking.
- Leadership tone: facts, impact, actions, decisions. Safe to forward to the CEO, Board or auditors.
- English spelling.

## Structure (must fit on ONE A4 page)
1. Navy banner with title and "Payroll | date | Draft for the management team".
2. In short: 3 to 4 sentences. What changed, how many people, headline cost.
3. Side-by-side table: Today | From [date] | Extra cost. Same staff, same hours.
   Rows: Pay, National Insurance the company pays, Pension the company pays, Total a year.
4. "What this means": annual and monthly cost, and whole-company change as a simple % ("less than 1p in every £1").
5. Who is affected (age, roles, teams, biggest group).
6. Costs still to come (items not yet in the total).
7. Action now (compliance fixes).
8. Decisions we need (numbered, 2 to 3 items).
9. Small italic notes line with assumptions.

## Design
- Hakim Group brand colours (from hakimgroup.co.uk):
  - Pink #DF5694 (headings, total row, table borders)
  - Navy #1E1C38 (banner, table header)
  - Text #302B4A
- Font Aptos 11 (notes 8.5 to 10). Word .docx.
- Narrow margins (about 1.2 cm top/bottom, 1.7 cm sides).
- Always render to PDF and confirm it is exactly one page before sending.

## Data rules
- Never commit staff data (payroll refs, pay, ages) to git. Keep files in the session scratchpad and send them to Khafayah.
- Cross-check totals with an independent calculation before writing the report.
- Ask before assuming missing rates (for example pension rate). Flag placeholders in the notes.
