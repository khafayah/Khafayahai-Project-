## 2. What the engagement data says

- Free to paid: 2 to 5 percent of a free audience usually converts to paid.
- Email list: owned email drives about 71 percent of webinar sign-ups. LinkedIn drives about 14 percent.
- Webinars: median sign-up to attendance is about 42 percent.
- Follow-up: attendees who got 4 or more personal emails within 14 days converted at 17.4 percent, against 9.1 percent without. (Single vendor source. Treat as a guide.)
- LinkedIn: document posts (PDF carousels) had the highest engagement in 2026, about 7 percent. Video views fell about 36 percent year on year.
- Community: members who take part are far more likely to stay and keep paying.

## 3. What the leaders do in common

1. One clear promise for one clear audience.
2. A free entry point: carousel, checklist, prompt pack or free workshop.
3. Email capture on every free item.
4. Regular live sessions. People pay for access to the expert, not for videos alone.
5. Recordings and templates, so busy people still get value.
6. A community space where members post their wins.
7. A clear ladder from free, to low-cost, to membership, to premium.

## 4. Recommended plan for Khafayah.AI

### Positioning
"Practical, safe AI for UK payroll teams and Muslim women in business. Taught by a Head of Payroll with 27 years of experience and a CIPP PAS Assessor."

### Suggested value ladder (prices to test, not final)

| Step | Offer | Suggested price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Weekly LinkedIn carousel plus email lead magnet, for example "10 safe AI prompts for payroll month-end" | Free | Grow the email list |
| Entry | 90-minute live workshop, recorded | £27 to £47 | First purchase, builds trust |
| Core | "AI for Payroll" live cohort or half-day course with CPD certificate | £149 to £249 | Undercuts £395 market rate, adds community |
| Recurring | Monthly membership: live build call, prompt library, Q&A | £29 to £49 per month | Recurring income |
| Premium | In-house team training for payroll departments | Quote per session | Highest value per sale |
| Partnership | Guest AI workshops for Sisters in Business, MWBN and similar | Fee or revenue share | Reach Muslim women founders fast |

### First 30 days
1. Build one lead magnet and one email welcome sequence (4 emails in 14 days).
2. Post two LinkedIn document carousels a week.
3. Run one free live workshop. Offer the paid workshop at the end.
4. Send the follow-up sequence to all sign-ups, including non-attendees.
5. Approach two Muslim women business networks with a guest workshop offer.
6. Track: list size, attendance rate, conversion rate, revenue.

### Compliance note
Run every payroll figure, rate or rule through the payroll fact-check process before it goes public.
