## 2. The gap Ilubirin fills

Most providers sit in one of two places:

1. Clinical one-to-one therapy. Private, costly, no ongoing sisterhood.
2. Retreats. Deep but one-off, and £700 or more.

Few UK offers found sit in the middle: an ongoing, Qur'an-centred, trauma-informed healing space that names emotional abuse, spiritual betrayal and cultural silence together. That middle space is where Ilubirin belongs.

Your credentials support it: qualified person-centred counsellor, trauma-informed practitioner, Islamic counselling and Islamic psychology CPD, supervisor, and author of the memoir She Returns.

## 3. How the leaders engage

- Instagram is the main shop window for Muslim women's therapy brands.
- Short reflections, Qur'an verses and gentle prompts get shared and saved.
- Retreats sell through sisterhood, stories and past attendee voices.
- Payment plans are common. Affordability matters to this audience.
- General creator data: 2 to 5 percent of a free audience goes on to pay. Owned email or WhatsApp lists convert far better than social media alone.

## 5. Ethics and safeguarding before you sell

Healing content for abuse survivors carries more risk than business training. Build these in from day one:

- Say clearly that groups and courses are not therapy. Point to Khafayah Counselling Ltd for one-to-one work.
- Keep therapy clients and course members separate. Check your professional body's guidance on dual relationships.
- Screen group members. Add a short intake form with risk questions.
- Put crisis signposting on every page and in every group, for example the Muslim Women's Network UK helpline and emergency services.
- Use a group agreement on confidentiality. WhatsApp groups show phone numbers. Use a WhatsApp Channel for broadcasts and a private platform for the group itself.
- Use trauma-informed marketing. No countdown pressure, no fear-based hooks, no "fix your life" promises.
- Get written consent for any story or testimonial. Check your professional body's rules on testimonials.
- Take the programme design to supervision.
- Check your insurance covers group and online work.

## 6. First 30 days

1. Write the 7-day email journaling series.
2. Post three reflections a week on Instagram. Share one into a WhatsApp Channel.
3. Run one free or low-cost online healing circle.
4. Invite circle attendees to a waiting list for the 8-week programme.
5. Draft the safeguarding pack: disclaimer, intake form, group agreement, signposting.
6. Approach the MCB directory, MCAPN and one retreat provider about listing or partnership.
